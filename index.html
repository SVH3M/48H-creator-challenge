The Rules

Read
Carefully.

A bet is only a bet if both sides are clear on the terms. This is the contract. Once it's signed, the only way out is to win — or to lose.

Disputes? The middleman holds the deposit. The timeline below is the ledger. The countdown is the judge.

01
One Video Per 48 Hours Each runner must publish at least one video by the end of every 48-hour window. Window 1 closes at the end of day 2 from race start.
02
$100 Monthly Stake Both runners deposit $100 to the middleman at the start of each month the race is active. No deposit, no race that month.
03
First Miss = Eliminated A missed deadline ends the runner's race. The opponent is declared the winner the moment the clock runs out — no make-ups, no make-good videos.
04
Winner Takes The Pot The middleman releases the entire accumulated deposit to the surviving runner immediately upon elimination of the other.
05
Double Miss = Race Voided If both runners miss the same deadline, the race is voided and the middleman returns deposits proportionally. (Don't let it come to this.)
